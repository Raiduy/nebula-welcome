The Fair Use Policy (FUP) has been outlined to safeguard the following:

* Ensure that our users can enjoy the highest level of performance and reliability when using our services.  
* Enforce legal use of our services by our users.  
* Protect our users and their/our equipment from harm.  
* Prevent legal action against our subscribers, company, directors, employees and associated companies.

At the Network Institute, we are committed to providing a safe and secure environment for our users. To achieve this, we have established a Fair Use Policy addressed to the users of our services (i.c. the Nebula Research Platform) that, for the sake of clarity, outlines the rules and guidelines as agreed in the Nebula Terms of Use. Hence, by using our services, you declare that you are aware of the mandatory compliance nature of the FUP and recognize the importance of abiding by the FUP and of using our services in a responsible and lawful manner.

The Network Institute reserves the right to modify this FUP at any time. Thus, you are highly recommended to review it regularly as it is your own responsibility to stay informed about our policies and ensure a safe and secure online experience for all users.

The Fair Use Policy is inextricably a part of the terms and condition, and therefore by subscribing and using our services you have accepted to adhere to this policy agreement.

# **1. Prohibited Activities**

The following is prohibited or limited when using our services:

* **Unauthorized Access**: Attempting to access or accessing any system, network, or data without authorization.

* **Malicious Activity**: Engaging in any activity that is intended to harm or disrupt our services.

* **Inappropriate Content:**  Using or generating any material (such as text, images, or videos) that is deemed unsuitable or offensive for a particular audience or context.

* **System Abuse**: Engaging in any activity that is intended to abuse or disrupt our systems, which is explicitly prohibited.

* **API usage** that is in conflict with this FUP, our Terms of Use or our Privacy Policy. Any application, being the software that is used or created by the user and connected to the Nebula Research Platform for a specific use case, is only used for said use case. Using API access to the Nebula Research Platform for purposes outside the said use case is prohibited.

* **Age Restrictions**: Use by minors (under the age of 18\) is not permitted.

* **Availability of the Platform**: Unless agreed upon in writing by the Parties, the Platform will only be available to users from organisations which are academic, research or non-profit/NGO.

* **Compliance with AI Regulations**:  Any use must comply with AI Regulations (European AI Act). Any high-risk and prohibited use is expressly prohibited.

* **Automated Decision-Making**: The Nebula Research Platform is not to be used for automated decisions with potentially significant consequences. As of yet, there are no mitigating measures in place to address problems with the provided AI models. The User acknowledges that AI outcomes may contain errors and biases. Since mitigating measures are not in place, unwanted consequences cannot be excluded. 

* **Protection of Private Information**: The User will never attempt to retrieve private information that may be present in the AI models provided by the Nebula Research Platform. See also our Privacy Policy

* **Data Handling and Security**: The User is responsible for handling their data wisely and takes into account that the Nebula Research Platform is a service in development. It is not permitted to make Highly Sensitive Data available to the Nebula Research Platform.

* **Oversee End-user compliance:** The participant institute shall oversee End-users’ compliance with the instructions and rules of use outlined in this FUP.

**Our Responsibilities and Obligations**

* Ensuring access to the Nebula Research Platform for authorised users.

* Designate and maintain an available contact person.

* Maintain sufficient technical capacity and knowledge to implement required updates.

* Handle provided API keys securely and responsibly.

# **2. Constraints for usage**

* This intended AI service is currently still in the pilot phase. Features, functionality and performance may be incomplete, in development or subject to change without notice.

* The intended AI service long-term support or maintenance is not guaranteed.

* Users may experience unexpected behavior, errors, or temporary outages.

* The Nebula Research Platform is set to be rate limited to balance the needs of all users, prevent overwhelming the system, and guarantee a responsive and reliable service.

# **3. Consequences of Non-Compliance**

* The Network Institute reserves the right to intervene at any time to maintain the integrity and availability of the Nebula Research Platform. In the event of a breach of the agreements, the Network Institute also reserves the right to block access to the Nebula Research Platform anytime when the Network Institute considers it necessary. The participating user will be notified.

* If the user uses the Nebula Research Platform incorrectly or unreasonably and this has significant consequences for the Nebula Research Platform, access to the Nebula Research Platform may be denied with immediate effect. In this case, the participating institute will be informed immediately.

# **4. Changes to this Policy**

The Network Institute reserves the right to modify this FUP at any time, and it is your responsibility to review it regularly to stay informed about our policies. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting on the Nebula Research Platform [legal documentation pages](/welcome/legalities), and your continued use of our services will be deemed acknowledgement of the revised FUP.

# **5. Contact Us**

If you have any questions or concerns about our FUP, please contact us at [nebula.cs@vu.nl](mailto:nebula.cs@vu.nl). We are committed to providing a safe and secure online environment for our users, and we appreciate your cooperation in helping us achieve this goal.
