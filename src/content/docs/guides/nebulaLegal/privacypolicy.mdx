We have implemented this Privacy Policy because your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) describes the type of information that the Network Institute (the “Institute”) gathers from users of the Nebula Research Platform (“Nebula”) services (the “Services”), and how the Institute uses that information.

The Policy is part of the Nebula Research Platform's Terms of Use. The Policy applies to all users of the Services (“Users”).

The Institute occasionally collects Personal Information from Users. “Personal Information” means any information that can, alone or associated with other information, be used to identify an individual, for instance by reference of a name, a username, an email address, an IP address, or others.

By using the Services, you consent to the terms of the Policy and to our processing of Personal Information in the manner and for the purposes set forth herein. If you do not agree with the Policy, please do not use the Services.

**Good to know**

Most AI providers quietly train new models on what you type, store your conversations on US or other non-EU servers under the CLOUD Act, and route your data through a long list of third-party processors. Nebula does none of that. Any information stays either on your local computer (the user’s computer) or on the Nebula servers on the Vrije Universiteit (VU) campus in Amsterdam, the Netherlands, and is never used to train a model, and is never shared with anyone.

We use open-source AI models that can be audited and verified. No black box algorithms or hidden data processing.

When using the SURF AI-Hub models through Nebula the data of your chat is exchanged with the SURF servers over a secured network connection. See <a href="https://servicedesk.surf.nl/wiki/spaces/WIKI/pages/248545389/Privacy+Policy](https://servicedesk.surf.nl/wiki/spaces/WIKI/pages/248545389/Privacy+Policy">SURF AI-Hub Terms of Use</a> for further information on their terms.

When using Knowledge Bases (KB(s)), the documents in the KB are stored on the Nebula servers on the VU-campus until such time you remove them or they are deleted when your account is disabled.

All data is encrypted in transit and using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.

# **1. Information We Collect**

The Institute collects the following information, some of which might be Personal Information.

**Information you provide directly**

The Institute collects information directly provided by Users as part of using the Services, such as: first name, email address, academic/support/student, department, faculty, intended use of Nebula, project description, link to project website, planned duration of Nebula use, planned using of sensitive data, budget available for Nebula use, member of the Social Analytics Lab (VU). We use this data to keep track of who is using Nebula, if we need further contact before giving you access \- in case of potential sensitive information \- and for generation of your account details.

At this moment this data is collected when you, as a potential user, requests access to our Services. This data is currently stored in a protected Google Drive folder only accessible by the Nebula administrators. 

The Institute generates a Nebula user name based on your first name, a Nebula email address and a password. This information is stored on the Nebula servers.

We retain your Information for as long as necessary to deliver the Services, to comply with any applicable legal requirements, to maintain security and prevent incidents and, in general, to pursue our legitimate interests.

You may decide to cancel your account and have any of your content deleted at any time by requesting the deletion of your profile at [nebula.cs@vu.nl](mailto:nebula.cs@vu.nl). After confirmation of the cancellation of your account, any data you might have stored on our servers will be deleted within 30 days after the confirmation of the cancellation of your account.

# **5. Data Security**

The security of your Personal Information is important to us. The Institute follows generally accepted industry standards, including the use of appropriate administrative, physical and technical safeguards, to protect Personal Information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage, is fully secure. Therefore, while the Institute strives to use acceptable means to protect Personal Information, the Institute cannot guarantee its absolute security or confidentiality. If you have any questions about security, you can contact the Institute at [nebula.cs@vu.nl](mailto:nebula.cs@vu.nl).

In the event of a personal‑data breach, the Network Institute (VU) will (i) assess the risk, (ii) notify the correct Privacy Champion at the VU within 72 hours, and (iii) inform affected users without undue delay when the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms.

Please be aware that certain Personal Information and other information provided by you in connection with your use of the Services may be stored on your device (even if that Information is not collected by the Institute). You are solely responsible for maintaining the security of your device from unauthorized access. Similarly, you also remain responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your password or any other information that should reasonably be held confidential.

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

* Hosted exclusively on VU-campus Amsterdam, Netherlands  
* SURF AI-Hub models are hosted on SURF computers in the Netherlands  
* TLS1.3 encryption for all data traffic and AES-128 (GCM SHA256 cypher) encryption  
* Encrypted daily backups with 24-hour recovery point objective  
* Regular vulnerability scans and annual penetration testing  
* Monitoring of invalid logins  
* Automatic server security updates  
* Manual system updates and reviews every 2 weeks  
* Rate limiting for preventing abuse  
* Remote access only via SSH key  
* Access Control:  
  * Physical Access to servers: the servers are currently located in our Lab space in the NU building on the VU-campus. Access is restricted to a very small vetted group of VU employees and any service personnel that has a legitimate right to access the room.
